🎨 Hand Gesture Drawing Canvas

Draw on screen using your hand gestures! No mouse or touch needed.

Simply point your finger to draw, make a fist to clear, and use gestures to control your canvas.

👆 Finger Drawing

Point your index finger to draw on the canvas

✊ Clear Canvas

Make a fist gesture to clear everything

✌️ Erase Mode

Show two fingers to enter erase mode

🎨 Color Change

Open palm gesture cycles through colors

📋 Complete Gesture Guide

  • 👆 One finger: Draw mode
  • ✌️ Two fingers: Erase mode
  • ✊ Closed fist: Clear canvas
  • 🖐️ Open palm: Change color
  • 👍 Thumbs up: Save drawing
  • 🔄 Wave hand: Undo last action

Tips: Make sure your hand is well-lit and clearly visible to the camera. Keep your fingers extended for best detection.
